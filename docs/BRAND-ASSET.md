# Badgers Research Explorer icon

Asset: `public/brand/badgers-research-explorer.png`

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The original badger face and open book form a custom project mark. The final edit replaces the blue book with Badger Red, using `#C5050C` from the [UW–Madison color guide](https://brand.wisc.edu/visual-identity/colors/) as the target. Transparency is preserved from the generated file.

The same asset is used by the shared brand component, landing-page preview, browser favicon, and Apple touch icon. Product name and asset path are centralized in `src/lib/brand.ts`.

## Initial generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original, polished, compact app icon for a student research-discovery website named Badgers Research Explorer. No text in the image. A clever geometric badger face, front-facing, with its characteristic two dark diagonal eye stripes; integrate the subtle silhouette of an open book into the lower face through simple negative space. Calm, intelligent, welcoming expression, not aggressive, not a sports mascot. A bespoke memorable logo, not clip art or a default UI glyph. Flat precise vector-like shapes, strong silhouette readable at 24 to 48 pixels, at most graphite (#25272D), restrained cobalt blue (#3C68BF), and opaque white highlights. Centered square composition, symbol fills roughly 85% of canvas; transparent outside the symbol, no background rectangle, no words, no letters, no compass, no magnifying glass, no gradation, no shadow, no 3D, no watermark. Entire icon visible with modest safe margins. Original design, do not copy the Wisconsin mascot or university marks. Output a clean production-ready transparent PNG icon.

## Final edit prompt

Edit this exact Badgers Research Explorer app icon. Change ONLY the blue open-book color to UW–Madison Badger Red, target hex #C5050C. Remove every blue accent. Preserve the original badger face, graphite and opaque white colors, all shapes, proportions, eyes, silhouette, book geometry, framing and transparent background. Use a clean solid red fill for the book, with no new texture or decorative elements. No text, no new symbols, no background, no redesign. Output a production-ready transparent PNG.
